GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Is UK Care Academy free?

Yes. The learning resources on this platform are designed to be free to access.

Will I receive an accredited UK care qualification?

No. This platform provides introductory learning and interview preparation. It is not an accredited qualification and does not replace employer induction, supervised practice or required workplace training.

Can I study if I have no care experience?

Yes. The introductory modules are designed to help beginners understand core care principles. Always answer employers honestly about your experience.

Do care rules differ across the UK?

Yes. Regulation and legal frameworks differ across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Lessons should identify the nation they apply to.
