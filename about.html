ABOUT US

Learning that opens doors.

Our aim is to make introductory care knowledge and interview preparation easier to access.

Our mission

UK Care Academy is a free educational resource for people exploring work in the UK care sector. We provide introductory lessons, practical learning prompts and interview preparation to help learners build confidence.

Our approach

We encourage dignity, respect, safeguarding, consent, inclusion and person-centred support. Learning here is a starting point, not a substitute for regulated qualifications, employer induction or supervised workplace practice.

Care law and regulation vary across the four UK nations. Always follow current local guidance, employer policies and the person's care plan.
